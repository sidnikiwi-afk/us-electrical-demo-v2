How we work

Three steps, no surprises

The same order every time, whether it is a tripping circuit at midnight or a rewire booked for next month.

Landlords and letting agents

Your EICR duty, in three numbers

Every installation in a rented property must be inspected and tested at intervals not exceeding five years. The tenant gets a copy of the report within 28 days. Local authorities can impose a financial penalty of up to thirty thousand pounds per breach.

  • 0Maximum inspection interval
  • 0To give the tenant their copy
  • 0Maximum penalty per breach

How the report reads

  • C1Danger presentRisk of injury. Made safe immediately.
  • C2Potentially dangerousRemedy within 28 days of the report.
  • C3Improvement recommendedNot a fail. Worth doing when convenient.
  • FIFurther investigationSomething needs looking at properly.

An EICR covers the fixed installation. It does not cover portable appliances, which are tested separately. Remedial work is quoted line by line alongside the report.
